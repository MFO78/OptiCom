1. Cassegrain Paraxial & Conic Prescription Geometry

                            

                                Primary focal length is f₁ = D₁ · (f/#₁) and system Effective Focal Length is EFL = m · f₁. For a chosen Back Working Distance (BWD = b), the secondary mirror intercept distance p₁ and mirror separation d are:
                            

                            

                                
p₁ = (f₁ + b) / (m + 1) ,  d = f₁ - p₁ ,  R₁ = -2·f₁ ,  R₂ = -2·m·p₁ / (m - 1)

                                
Classical: k₁ = -1.0 ,  k₂ = -((m + 1)/(m - 1))²  [Zero Spherical, Coma present]

                                
Ritchey-Chrétien (RC): k₁ < -1 , k₂ < k₂_class  [Aplanatic: Zero Spherical & Zero Coma]

                            

                        


                        

                            
2. Seeing (θ) vs. Fried Parameter (r₀) & Wavelength Scaling

                            

                                By Roddier (1981) and Fried (1966), long-exposure astronomical seeing FWHM is θ(λ) = 0.98 · λ / r₀(λ). Astronomical sites quote seeing and r₀ at λ₀ = 500 nm, where 0.98 × 500nm × 206265″/rad = 10.107″·cm:
                            

                            

                                
At 500 nm:  θ_500 [arcsec] = 10.107 / r₀_500 [cm]   (e.g. r₀ = 10 cm ⇔ θ = 1.01″)

                                
At Laser λ: r₀(λ) = r₀_500 · (λ / 500nm)^(6/5)      (e.g. at 1550nm, r₀ = 3.89 × r₀_500)

                                
At Laser λ: θ(λ)  = 0.98 · λ / r₀(λ) = θ_500 · (λ / 500nm)^(-1/5)  (at 1550nm, θ = 0.80 × θ_500)

                            

                            

                                Key Insight: Because r₀ ∝ λ^(1.2) grows faster than λ¹, angular seeing θ(λ) ∝ λ^(-0.2) actually improves by ~20% at 1550 nm compared to 500 nm, while turbulence ratio D₁/r₀(λ) drops by almost 4×!
                            

                        


                        

                            
3. Adaptive Optics (AO) Noll Zernikes & Servo-Lag Floor

                            

                                
Uncorrected (N=0): σ²_0 = 1.0299 · (D₁/r₀(λ))^(5/3) | Tip/Tilt (N=2): σ²_fit = 0.1340 · (D₁/r₀(λ))^(5/3)

                                
High-Order (N>5):  σ²_fit = 0.2944 · N^(-√3/2) · (D₁/r₀(λ))^(5/3) [rad²]

                                
AO Servo + WFS Floor: σ²_servo ≈ 0.055 · (D₁/r₀(λ))^(5/3) + 0.035 [rad²] (for N > 0)

                                
Net Strehl Ratio:  S_net = exp(-(σ²_fit + σ²_servo)) · (1 - ε²)

                            

                        


                        

                            
4. Realistic 99%-Availability FSOC Link Budget & Thermal Despace

                            

                                
G_Tx = 10·log₁₀((π·D_Tx/λ)²) - 1.8 dB (Clipped Gaussian) | L_optics = -3.5 dB (Coatings/Splitters)

                                
L_ptg(99%) = 4.343 · 2 · (-ln(0.01)) · (σ_j / θ_e2)² | L_scint(99%) = 1.8 + 4.2·(D₁/r₀)^0.45 · Avg(D₁)

                                
Thermal Focus Shift: ΔBWD = (m² + 1) · d · CTE · ΔT  vs.  Quarter-Wave DoF = ±2·λ·(f/#_eff)²

                            

                        


                        

                            
5. Tertiary Flat Mirror (M₃) & Nasmyth Port Geometry

                            

                                Placing a -45° flat fold mirror (M₃) centered on the optical axis at (z₃, 0) with surface equation z + y = z₃ reflects the converging beam by 90° downward (-Y) when M₃ intercepts the beam ahead of the unfolded focus:
                            

                            

                                
2D Reflection Mapping across M₃:   (z', y') = (z₃ - y , z₃ - z)

                                
Unfolded Focus (b, y_img) → Folded: z_N = z₃ - y_img ,   y_N = -(b - z₃)

                                
On-Axis Path to Nasmyth Focus:     L_N = b - z₃   (Vertical Throw h_N = -y_N = b - z₃)

                                
Beam Footprint Minor Axis (D₃_min): D₃_min = D₂_axial · (L_N / L₂) + 2 · (d + z₃) · tan(θ_FOV)

                                
45° Elliptical Major Axis (D₃_maj): D₃_maj = √2 · D₃_min   | Tube Clearance: ΔR = h_N - (D₁ / 2)
